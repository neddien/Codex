	- [TODO]: Add flip_x/flip_y to Sprite and swap the UVs in RenderBatch::upload_quad.
	          Negative transform scale must never be used to mirror anything at or above a
	          rigid body: construct_physics_body extracts the angle with atan2(world[0].y,
	          world[0].x), and a mirrored transform has a negative determinant, so the angle
	          comes out 180deg off and handedness (CCW/CW) inverts. Box2D cannot represent
	          mirrored bodies at all. Flip in the renderer instead.

	          Collider sizing is already sign-agnostic (uses glm::length), only the angle breaks,
	          which is why this fails silently.
